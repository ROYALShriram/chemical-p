About this project

Curiosity makes chemistry click.

ChemLife is an educational website designed to connect classroom chemistry with the products and materials we use every day.

Student project
🎯

Project purpose

The purpose of this website is to make everyday chemistry easier to understand. It introduces familiar substances, shows their chemical formulas, explains common uses and origins, and highlights both benefits and possible risks.

What we learned

Many household products contain useful chemical substances, but safe use depends on concentration, exposure, correct handling, and following product instructions. Natural does not always mean harmless, and synthetic does not automatically mean dangerous.

Conclusion

Chemistry helps us make informed choices.

Everyday chemistry is not limited to a laboratory: it is present in food, cleaning, personal care, health, and the materials around us. Knowing the composition and purpose of common substances helps us appreciate their advantages while recognizing limitations and safety concerns. By combining simple explanations with an interactive quiz, this project encourages scientific curiosity, responsible product use, and lifelong learning.

Educational content only. Chemical formulas and descriptions are simplified for introductory learning; always rely on product labels and trusted safety guidance for real-world use.
